SUPPORT

Need help with FitGrade? Find answers below or email us. We reply within 24 hours.

How do I get a trainer code?

Trainers: open Profile & Settings in the app. Your 6-digit trainer code is shown there, and you can share it from the same screen. Athletes enter that code after signing up to join your roster. The code is free and works as long as your account is active.

How does an athlete join a trainer?

Sign up as an athlete, choose that you have a trainer, and enter the 6-digit code your trainer gave you. You appear on their roster automatically. Athletes without a trainer can skip this step and use FitGrade on their own.

How do I log a session?

Trainers: open a client from your roster and tap Log Session. Set each body part's grade, add a session note, and save. Athletes can log their own self-assessment from their home screen. Every session is saved to the client's history.

How do I record an injury?

On the Log Session screen, tap Flag next to the affected body part, then choose a severity (Mild, Moderate, or Severe) and add a note. Flagged injuries are marked on the client's body map and are included when you generate a plan.

How does the AI training plan work?

Open a client and tap Generate AI Plan. FitGrade builds a 7-day plan from the client's grades, targets, injuries, and your notes. AI plans are suggestions, not medical advice.

What is included in the free plan?

The free plan includes up to 3 clients, session logging, injury tracking, and AI plans. FitGrade Pro adds unlimited clients and advanced features.

How do I subscribe, cancel, or restore FitGrade Pro?

Subscribe from the upgrade screen in the app. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose FitGrade Pro. To restore a previous purchase, tap Restore Purchase on the upgrade screen. Billing is handled by Apple.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and we will send you a reset link. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile & Settings → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently removes your account and data. If you cannot open the app, email us from your account email and we will delete it for you.
